My IT support process

A structured process, every time — so problems get fixed and stay fixed.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Identify the user's issue and requirements.

  2. 02

    Diagnose

    Investigate symptoms and identify the root cause.

  3. 03

    Troubleshoot

    Apply a structured troubleshooting process.

  4. 04

    Resolve

    Implement the appropriate solution.

  5. 05

    Verify

    Test the system and confirm the issue is resolved.

  6. 06

    Document

    Record the solution and recommendations.
